I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Ge Bai and co-supervised by Xin Wang. I received my BSc in Mathematics from The University of Hong Kong in 2025.

My research interests include quantum information and computing, quantum error correction, and AI for quantum information and computing theory.

I am also a principal developer of [Lean-QIT](https://github.com/QuAIR/Lean-QIT), an open-source Lean 4 formal infrastructure for quantum information theory.
